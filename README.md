## The "Auditor's Eye" Methodology

Don't just guess whether you are compliant; know for sure. Because we enforce management system standards daily as active Lead Auditors, we simulate the exact scrutiny your organisation will face on certification day—interpreting compliance "grey areas" and exposing non-conformities *before* your formal Stage 1, Stage 2, or surveillance audit.

Every engagement follows a structured three-stage remote framework:
1. **Pre-Audit Scope & Document Sampling:** Reviewing manuals, Statements of Applicability (SoAs), risk treatment matrices, and prior audit records.
2. **Live Remote Audit Simulation:** Interactive video conferencing and screen-sharing sessions testing live operational evidence ("retrieval" over boilerplate documentation).
3. **Lead Auditor Reporting & CAP Delivery:** Evidence-backed reports classifying Major NCs, Minor NCs, and OFIs with prioritised Corrective Action Plans (CAPs).
